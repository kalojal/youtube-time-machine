# 🕰️ YouTube Time Machine (v1.0)

Travel back to any exact date and time on YouTube! **YouTube Time Machine** sets a real-time virtual clock in your browser, filters out videos uploaded after your chosen date, scales view counts and upload ages to match your selected era, and builds a personalized historical recommendation feed.

---

## ✨ Features
* **Real-Time Virtual Clock:** Pick any past date and time and watch it tick forward second by second.
* **Future Video & Shorts Blocker:** Automatically hides videos, live streams, Mixes, and Shorts uploaded after your virtual timestamp.
* **Clickable Historical Dates & Views:** Scales modern view counts and relative ages to match your chosen year—click any date or view count to toggle between the virtual estimate and actual modern numbers.
* **Era-Accurate Search & Recommendations:** Automatically injects `before:YYYY-MM-DD` into every YouTube search and learns topics from the videos you watch to build a custom **Recommended Feed** bar.
* **Temporary Local Comments:** Post classic unsaved comments on watch pages that disappear on refresh.
* **Works With Classic Layout Extensions:** Pairs cleanly with extensions like **CustomTube** or **Vorapis V3** if you want classic 2009–2018 visual themes.

---

## 🚀 How to Install (Free - Takes 30 Seconds)
1. Click the green **Code** button at the top of this page and select **Download ZIP**.
2. Unzip the downloaded folder onto your computer.
3. Open Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions`.
4. Turn on **Developer mode** (toggle switch in the top-right corner).
5. Click **Load unpacked** and select the unzipped folder.
6. Pin **YouTube Time Machine** to your toolbar, pick any date and time, and click **Travel to This Time**!
